# The reveal

When the card arrives, Capsule shows the back first. Large titles then appear one at a time: year, grade, then rarity. Each title settles into the caption under the card. The card then flips to the front. Buyback and Double appear after the flip.

A title is skipped when that fact is missing. Capsule does not invent a year or a grade.

Insured value and the buyback quote appear with the choice, after the flip. The buyback quote is the amount locked for that open. It is not 85% of the pack price unless that is what the quote actually is.

Refreshing a card you have already revealed does not play the sequence again in that tab. A reduced-motion setting skips the motion and shows the front.

While the card is still loading, the screen keeps a dark slab. It does not show empty Year, Grade, or Rarity rows.
