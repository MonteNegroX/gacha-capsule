# Capsule

{% hint style="warning" %}
Draft, written 12 October 2026. These pages are not published on GitBook. They describe the live $25 pack on [capsulepull.xyz](https://www.capsulepull.xyz).
{% endhint %}

Capsule opens one graded card pack for 25 USDG. You see the card, then you buy it back or Double a Common. A won Double also lists that card as a 7-day loan. The card stays in the Capsule vault. It is not sent to your wallet.

Packs priced above $25 are on the floor and marked Coming soon. They do not take payment.
