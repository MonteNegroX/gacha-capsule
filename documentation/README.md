# Capsule

{% hint style="warning" %}
Draft, written 12 October 2026. These pages are not published on GitBook. They describe the live packs on [capsulepull.xyz](https://www.capsulepull.xyz).
{% endhint %}

Capsule has two packs you can open.

The $25 pack, `pokemon_25`, is one graded card. Pay 25 USDG on Robinhood Chain, or 25 USDC on Hyperliquid. You see the card, then you take the buyback or Double a Common. A won Double lists that card as a 7-day loan. The card stays in the Capsule vault.

The Garage Pack, `garage_20`, costs 20 USDG or 20 USDC. It opens on Solana. The car is sold there. The buyback is paid in USDG on Robinhood. That pack has no Double and no loan.

A buyback or a refund is Robinhood USDG, sent to the wallet that paid. That includes a pack paid in Hyperliquid USDC.

Packs priced above $25 are on the floor and marked Coming soon. They do not take payment.
