# Open a pack

The open pack is `pokemon_25`. The price is 25 USDG on Robinhood Chain.

1. Connect the wallet that will pay.
2. Choose the $25 pack. Packs above $25 stay on Coming soon.
3. Send 25 USDG to the Capsule treasury. The token and the treasury address are on [Contracts](contracts.md).
4. Wait for the card. The reveal starts when the card is known. See [The reveal](the-reveal.md).
5. Choose Buyback or Double. You have 24 hours. If you do not choose, Capsule runs the buyback for you.

One payment opens one pack. A second payment is a second open. Finishing an order in one browser tab does not lock every other tab.

{% hint style="info" %}
Open stays off when the vault cannot cover a pack. Refresh and try again once the vault is ready. You are not charged for a click that never starts an order.
{% endhint %}

You can watch the reveal without paying at [capsulepull.xyz/?preview=reveal](https://www.capsulepull.xyz/?preview=reveal). The preview buttons do not send a transaction.
