# What is not live

These pages should not claim the following.

* Packs above $25. They are visible and marked Coming soon. They do not take 50, 80, or any higher price.
* A Hyperliquid open. The Robinhood USDG path is the one that runs.
* A player wallet receiving the Solana NFT on a buyback or a Double win.
* An annual yield. The 3% and 1% are term fees on a repaid loan. See [Money](money.md).
* A DefiLlama fees page. The adapter was merged. The public page was still absent on 12 October 2026.
* A DefiLlama TVL page. That listing is a separate open request and is not merged.
* This GitBook site. The files in `documentation/` are a draft. See [Publishing this space](publishing.md).
