# Buyback and Double

After the reveal you choose one of two actions. The names stay the same on the button and in the result.

## Buyback

Buyback pays the quote locked at open. The payment is the amount the buyback actually returns, in USDG, to the wallet that paid for the pack. The card stays in the Capsule vault.

If you do not choose within 24 hours, Capsule runs this buyback for you.

## Double

Double is offered only when the card is Common.

Capsule rolls a number from 0 through 99.

* Below 49, you win. Capsule pays 2× the buyback quote locked at open, and lists that same card as a 7-day loan. See [Lend](lend.md).
* 49 or above, you lose. You receive nothing. Capsule keeps the card.

A win that nobody funds within 2 days is cancelled and sold back to Collector Crypt. A win that is funded stays a loan for the term. A loss is not sold on that 2-day timer.

The card does not go into a loan unless Double wins. Buyback does not list the card.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The Solana NFT is not transferred to you on a win or on a buyback. It stays in the Capsule vault until a loan default sends it to the funder who asks for it.
{% endhint %}
