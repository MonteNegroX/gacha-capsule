# Money

Two different flows move USDG. They are not the same number.

## Pack open

You pay 25 USDG. If you take Buyback, you receive the quote locked at open. If you win Double, you receive 2× that quote. If you lose Double, you receive nothing.

The pack price is not a protocol fee. The buyback is a payment to you, not a yield.

## Loan

On a repaid loan the funder earns 3% of principal and Capsule earns 1% of principal. The principal itself goes back to the funder. It is not a fee.

Those percents are for the 7-day term. They are not an annual percentage yield. A loan that is never funded, or that ends in default, does not pay the 3% or the 1%.

On the fees record, volume is principal that was funded. Fees are the 4% collected when a loan is repaid: 3% to the funder and 1% to Capsule. Dollars come from closed loans.
